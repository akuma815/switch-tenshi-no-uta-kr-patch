[2편 최신 영상 보존·복원 안내](https://github.com/akuma815/switch-tenshi-no-uta-2-kr-patch/blob/main/docs/VIDEO_RECOVERY_2026-10-16.md), [검증 원장](https://github.com/akuma815/switch-tenshi-no-uta-2-kr-patch/blob/main/docs/video_git_verification_2026-10-16.json).
